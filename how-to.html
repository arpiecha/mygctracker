1
Open Safari and go to your dashboard
Open Safari (not Chrome) on your iPhone and navigate to your GCTracker dashboard link. Tap the share button — the box with an arrow pointing up — at the bottom of the screen.
2
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the share sheet that appears and tap "Add to Home Screen". It shows a plus icon next to it.
3
Name it and tap "Add"
You can rename it if you like — "GCTracker" or your project name works great. Tap "Add" in the top right corner to confirm.
4
Done — it's on your home screen!
The GCTracker icon will appear on your home screen. Tap it anytime to open your dashboard instantly, just like a real app.
Must use Safari

The "Add to Home Screen" option only appears in Safari on iPhone. If you're using Chrome, switch to Safari first.

1
Open Chrome and go to your dashboard
Open Google Chrome on your Android phone and navigate to your GCTracker dashboard link.
2
Tap the three dots menu
Tap the three vertical dots in the top right corner of Chrome to open the browser menu.
3
Tap "Add to Home screen"
Scroll down in the menu and tap "Add to Home screen". Chrome may also show a banner at the bottom automatically.
4
Tap "Add" to confirm
A dialog will appear asking you to confirm. Tap "Add" and the GCTracker icon will appear on your home screen.
Tip

On some Android devices Chrome will automatically show a banner saying "Add GCTracker to Home screen" — just tap that instead of going through the menu.
